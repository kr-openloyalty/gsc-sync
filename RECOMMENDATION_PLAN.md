Date: 2026-07-30
Scope: Bottom-of-funnel keywords, US market. Data: Ahrefs (volume/KD/CPC/SERP) +
Google Search Console (last 14 days, 2026-07-14 → 07-27, `sc-domain:openloyalty.io`).
Excludes the 13 head terms specified by the client.

## How each keyword was scored
Four signals, combined:
1. **Commercial value** — CPC and whether paid ads are live on the SERP (real money moving).
2. **Winnability** — Keyword Difficulty + current position (buried > net-new for speed).
3. **Intent** — BOFU (comparison / vendor-seeking / cost).
4. **Whitespace** — is a competitor already there and OL absent.

Note: every target SERP carries an AI Overview → clicks are structurally near-zero
(0 clicks on all 20 terms in 14 days). Measure on **position + impressions + citation
share**, not clicks.
